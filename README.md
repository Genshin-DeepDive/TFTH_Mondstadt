# **ABOUT *Tales from the Hoyoverse***
<sub>Written by [u/Genshin_DeepDive](https://www.reddit.com/user/Genshin_DeepDive/)</br>
Last Updated 11 January 2026</sub>
</br></br>

*Tales from the Hoyoverse* is an all-encompassing fan project designed to serve as written (and eventually audible) archive of the Hoyoverse IPs, including *Genshin Impact*, *Honkai: Impact 3rd*, *Honkai: Star Rail*, *Zenless Zone Zero*, and others.
Our goal is to collate all of the actions, audio, and visuals in the Hoyoverse games into written and audiobook-style formats, which are ideal for archiving.
This means converting almost exclusively visual and audible content into written prose, and then eventually providing audio forms of that prose.

There are **huge** benefits to undertaking this sort of project.
  1. *MiHoYo's content is primarily SAaS (Software as a Service).*
     This means that there will, eventually, come a time when the servers are shut down for their IPs, and the content is retired.
     Fan efforts will live on in the form of fanart and video archives, but this form of content has slightly more permanence given the fragility of our modern internet.
  2. *Not everyone is privileged or abled enough to play MiHoYo games (or games in general).*
     Folks with debilitating disabilities or those who find video games excessively challenging, frustrating, or inaccessible are often excluded from participating in gaming experiences.
     They are often forced to rely on video playthroughs or cutscene compilations as well as fan-built game wikis (such as this excellent Genshin Impact wiki).
     This content is rich with IP but in a format that is unfriendly to someone wanting to "experience the content as it was originally intended."
     Our goal is to create flowing written and audiobook content that flows as if you were experiencing the game in a different form, which opens up gaming to folks in the disabled or less-privileged categories.
  3. *MiHoYo properties were likely created from sprawling design documentation and are thus well-suited to book/audiobook form.*
     It is highly likely that, as part of the development process, MiHoYo took the time to distill their content into concise yet well-written design documentation.
     It would be thrilling to obtain those documents, but it's unlikely they'll ever see the light of day.
     Reading through a transformative work that utilizes the direct dialog and actions of the IP serves a near-identical purpose and is even more enjoyable to read.
  4. *This project serves as transformative content that is worth pouring heart and soul into, without stepping on copyright or trademark toes.*
     It's extremely challenging to "transform" content you love without stealing from the original creator.
     However, creating prosaic forms of the original content allows us to focus on the canon events, characters, and locations while using our writing abilities to transform the visuals into written form... and I do mean more than just dialog or in-game books.
